Yetera

Not yet. Give it time. Then decide.

Yetera is a quiet place to think through a purchase. Save what you want, let it wait, then come back and decide — buy it or let it go.

How it works

  1. 1

    Save

    Put the thing you want here first, with its price and the reason you want it.

  2. 2

    Wait

    Give it a waiting period that suits you. You can still decide early if you want to.

  3. 3

    Decide

    Come back when the time is up. Buying it or letting it go — either is a considered choice.

Yetera doesn’t stop you from buying things, and it doesn’t judge your choices. It just keeps a little distance between “I want this now” and “I’ve actually thought about it.”

About Yetera

  • Your records stay on this device. There is no account to create. You can sync with the Apple account signed in on your device, or use Yetera entirely offline. Photos always stay on the device.
  • What you let go doesn’t disappear. It builds into a history you can look back on, and you can set a goal for something you’re saving toward.
  • No ads, and no behavioural tracking. There is no server of our own, and your records are not uploaded to any Yetera-operated endpoint.
  • Your first 3 completed decisions are free. One-time unlock after that, with no subscription. The price is shown by the App Store for your region.

What Yetera isn’t Yetera isn’t a budgeting or investing tool, and it doesn’t score your spending. It doesn’t promise to save you money — it only makes room for the decision.

To see how your data is handled, read the Privacy Policy. For help, visit Support.
